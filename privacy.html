Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Hfad is a free flashcard app. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared.

Without an account

Your decks, cards and progress are saved only in your browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

With a Google account

If you sign in, Hfad keeps a copy of your decks, cards and progress in your account so they follow you on every device. To do this it stores:

  • the basics Google sign-in gives us: your name, email address and a user id;
  • your decks, cards, study progress and daily study summaries.

This data is stored by Google Firebase, a service of Google. Only you can read or change it: the database refuses everyone else, including other users.

Other services

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its sign-in library from Google, so Google can see your IP address when the page loads, like on most websites. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

In Hfad, open Settings › Delete my account. This deletes your saved data and your account for good. To remove the cards kept on a device, clear the site's data in your browser settings.
